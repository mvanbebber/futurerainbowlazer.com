Privacy

Last updated October 29, 2026

Slow Down Buddy is built so your music stays on your device. Here is exactly what happens with your information.

Your audio never leaves your device

  • Songs you open, recordings you make, and your loops, markers and settings are stored in your browser on your device (using the browser's built-in storage). They aren't uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Recording a browser tab or your microphone only happens when you start it and approve it in your browser's prompt.
  • Backups are files you save yourself; we never see them.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-up.

Anonymous usage counts

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count visits and which features get used (for example “opened an MP3” or “made a loop”). It doesn't use cookies and doesn't collect personal information such as your name or email. It records things like the page visited, the referring site, browser and operating system, screen size and country. Ad and tracker blockers may block it, and that's fine.

Feedback and the Pro waitlist

If you send feedback or join the Pro waitlist, we receive what you type in the form (your message, and your email if you give it). The feedback form also includes your browser details, the app's current speed setting and the kind of song you have open (a file, a recording or the demo, never the song itself) to help us fix problems. These forms are handled by our host, Netlify. We use your email only to reply to you or to tell you when Pro launches, and we don't sell or share it. To have your submission deleted, send a request through the feedback form.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify, which, like any web host, processes technical information such as IP addresses to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Links to other sites

The Support button opens Venmo, and some pages link to other sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Please don't submit personal information through the forms if you're under 13.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top will change too.
