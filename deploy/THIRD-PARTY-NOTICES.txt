The minified decoder files are byte-for-byte the "dist" builds published on npm (opus-decoder.min.js with its
source-map comment removed). Their unmodified source packages are available at
https://slowdownbuddy.com/licenses/source/ (SHA-256 checksums in SHA256SUMS.txt).
