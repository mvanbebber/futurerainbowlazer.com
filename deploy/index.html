    Record audio from any website

    Slow Down Buddy can record whatever a website is playing and save it straight to your library, ready to slow down, loop and transpose. On a computer, open the page with the music in another tab of Chrome, Edge or Brave (or another Chrome-based browser), press the screen button in the toolbar, choose that tab, switch on “Share tab audio”, and play the part you want. Press “Stop and add to library” when you’re done. The microphone button records anything you can play out loud, on any device.

    On a phone or tablet, use its screen recorder, then open the video here: Slow Down Buddy keeps just the audio. iPhone and iPad: add Screen Recording in Settings → Control Center, swipe down to Control Center, tap the record button, play the song, then stop from the red indicator at the top; the video is saved in Photos. Android: pull down Quick Settings, tap Screen record and choose to record media or device audio. Then tap the folder button and pick the video (on iPhone, choose Photo Library). YouTube and most websites work; Spotify and Apple Music record silence on phones.

    Sound check

    Checking…

    No beep: the device is muting the page. Check the ring/silent switch and the volume buttons, and close other apps that are playing sound. Beep works but songs are silent: switch off the high-quality engine and press Play again.

    Put Slow Down Buddy on your Home Screen

    It opens full screen like an app, and your library is safer there: Safari can clear a website’s data after 7 days without a visit, but not a Home Screen app’s.

    1. Back up your songs. The Home Screen app starts with its own empty library, so you’ll bring them over with this file.
    3. Bring your songs over. Open Slow Down Buddy from your Home Screen, tap the Library button, then Restore backup and pick the backup file (in Files, under Downloads).

    Try it: tap ▶ to play the demo, then tap 50% to hear it slowed down without changing pitch. Tap Loop start and Loop end to loop a passage.

    Record from any website or app: use your phone’s screen recorder (on iPhone, Screen Recording in Control Center), then open the video with the folder button. YouTube and most websites work; Spotify and Apple Music record silence.

    Recording a website needs Chrome, Edge or Brave on a computer. This browser can’t capture a tab’s sound. Here you can open a file, or play the song out loud and record it with the microphone.
