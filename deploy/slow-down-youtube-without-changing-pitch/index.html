Slow Down Buddy Open the app

Slow down YouTube, or any website, without changing pitch

Slow Down Buddy records whatever a browser tab is playing and saves it to your practice library. From there you can slow it down to 25% without the chipmunk or the drag, loop a few bars, and change the key.

Open Slow Down Buddy, freeNo download, no sign-up. Works on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone and iPad.

On a computer (Chrome, Edge or Brave)

  1. Open slowdownbuddy.com in one tab and the video or song in another.
  2. In Slow Down Buddy, press the record a website button (the screen icon) in the toolbar.
  3. Choose the tab with the music and switch on Share tab audio.
  4. Play the part you want to learn, then press Stop and add to library.
  5. Pick a speed (try 50% or 70%), press L at the start of the hard bit and L at the end to loop it, and play along.
Tip: record a bit more than you need. Trimming happens with loops and markers, so nothing is lost.

Everything else in Slow Down Buddy (slowing down, loops, markers, transpose, EQ) works in all of these browsers, including Safari and Firefox, on computers, phones and tablets.

On iPhone, iPad or Android

Phone and tablet browsers can't record another tab, but your phone's built-in screen recorder can, and Slow Down Buddy opens the video and keeps just the audio:

  • iPhone and iPad: add Screen Recording in Settings → Control Center. Swipe down to Control Center, tap the record button, play the video, then stop from the red indicator at the top. In Slow Down Buddy, tap the folder button, choose Photo Library and pick the recording.
  • Android: pull down Quick Settings, tap Screen record and choose to record media or device audio, then open the video with the folder button.
  • YouTube and most websites record fine. Spotify, Apple Music and some other apps mute their sound in screen recordings; for those, use the microphone button:
  • The microphone button: play the song out loud, on another device or a speaker, and record it.
  • Files you already have: MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV and voice memos open directly, even Apple Lossless and Opus files that iPhone browsers usually can't play.

Why it sounds better slowed down

Most "slow down" tools smear drum hits and plucked notes into a swishy blur at low speeds. Slow Down Buddy finds every attack in the song and keeps it sharp, while stretching the notes in between, so a cowbell still sounds like a cowbell at 25%.

Play fair

Recordings stay on your device for your own practice. Respect the rights of the artists and sites you record from; some protected streams can't be recorded and come through silent.

Questions

Does slowing down change the pitch?

No. Speed and pitch are separate: you can slow down to 25% and keep the original key, or transpose separately by semitones and cents.

Do I need a browser extension?

No. Recording a tab uses a feature built into Chrome, Edge and Brave. Nothing to install.

Which browsers can record a website?

Chrome, Edge and Brave on a computer, and other Chrome-based browsers such as Opera, Vivaldi and Arc should work too. Safari, Firefox and phone browsers cannot capture another tab. On a phone or tablet, use its screen recorder and open the video in Slow Down Buddy; on a computer, use the microphone button.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording, slowing down and storage all happen in your browser on your device.

How slow can it go?

Down to 25% speed, and up to 200%.
