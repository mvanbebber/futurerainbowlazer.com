Slow Down Buddy vs Amazing Slow Downer

Amazing Slow Downer has been a go-to for years, and on iPhone it can play straight from your music library. But it's sold separately for each platform, and the free Lite version only plays part of each song. Slow Down Buddy is free on every device at once, keeps attacks crisp at very slow speeds, and adds markers, saved loops, EQ presets and recording from a website tab.

Slow Down Buddy vs Capo

Capo is a beautifully made Apple app with automatic chord and beat detection, sold as a yearly subscription for Mac and iOS. If you're on Windows, Android or a Chromebook, or you don't want a subscription, Slow Down Buddy gives you slow-down, loops, markers, transpose and EQ for free in the browser. Automatic chords and bar markers are on our list for a future Pro version.

Slow Down Buddy vs Transcribe!

Transcribe! is the classic desktop transcription tool: a one-time license, video support and deep note analysis. If you want something that also works on your phone, needs no install and costs nothing, Slow Down Buddy covers the everyday practice workflow. See the full Transcribe! comparison.

Slow Down Buddy vs Anytune

Anytune focuses on Apple devices. Slow Down Buddy runs the same way on Apple, Windows, Android and Chromebook, so your practice setup isn't tied to one ecosystem, and you can move your whole library between devices with one backup file.

Slow Down Buddy vs Moises

Moises is built around AI: it separates a song into vocals, drums, bass and more, and detects chords and tempo, with the best features behind a subscription and an account. If you mainly need to slow down, loop and transpose, Slow Down Buddy does that free, with no account and without uploading your music. Its center-cancel and EQ presets go a long way toward isolating a part.

When another app is the better pick

  • You need AI stem separation: try Moises.
  • You want automatic chord charts on Mac or iPhone: try Capo.
  • You transcribe from video or want spectrum analysis on a desktop: try Transcribe!.

For everything else, from slowing down a solo to looping a bass fill on your phone between lessons, Slow Down Buddy is free and already open in your browser.

Questions

Is Slow Down Buddy really free?

Yes. Slowing down, looping, markers, transpose, EQ, recording and backups are all free with no sign-up. A Pro version with extras such as exporting slowed-down audio is planned; today's features stay free.

Does it work on Windows and Android?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser, including Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on Windows, Mac, Chromebook, Android, iPhone and iPad.

Can I import my songs from another app?

Open the audio files directly: MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A (AAC, Apple Lossless and Opus), the audio of MP4 and MOV videos, and voice memos. Loops and markers from other apps don't carry over.

Does it separate vocals and instruments like Moises?

Not with AI. It has center-cancel to reduce the vocal, left or right channel only, and EQ presets for bass, guitar, vocals and drums. AI separation is something we may add later.

Is my music uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser, and your songs are stored on your device.
